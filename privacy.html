Privacy Policy

Effective as of October 24, 2026

Sunshine Rewards (“we”) is a free app where you complete simple offers and earn Sun Coins. This page explains what information we collect, how we use it and your choices. By using the app you agree to this policy.

Information We Collect

When you use Sunshine Rewards we collect only what we need to run the app:

  • Google account details: your name, email address, profile photo and a unique user ID, received when you sign in with Google.
  • Account activity: your coin balance, daily check-in streak, spin chances, referral code and who referred you.
  • Proofs you submit: the registered email ID, mobile number and screenshot you upload for an offer.
  • Withdrawal details: your name and your UPI ID or bank account number and IFSC code. We use these only to pay you.
  • Technical data: device and browser type, IP address and error logs, collected through Google and Firebase.

How We Use Your Information

  • Create and manage your account.
  • Verify offers you complete and credit your rewards.
  • Process your withdrawal requests.
  • Prevent fraud, fake proofs, duplicate accounts and misuse.
  • Give you support and improve the app.

Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share the details you submit as proof (such as your registered email or mobile number) with the partner company of an offer, only to confirm that you completed it. We may also share information when the law requires it or to protect our users and our service.

Third-Party Services

The app uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify you:

  • Google Sign-In and Google Authentication
  • Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore (to store your account data)
  • GitHub Pages (to host the app)
  • Monetag, our advertising network (see Advertising below)
  • Partner companies and their apps. Each partner has its own privacy policy and we are not responsible for it.

Advertising

Sunshine Rewards is a free app supported by ads. We show ads through the Monetag advertising network. Monetag may collect information such as your IP address, device and browser details and an advertising identifier, and may use cookies, to show ads and measure how they perform. We do not give Monetag your name, email or payment details. See Monetag’s own privacy policy for how it handles data.

We never ask you to click on ads. Please do not click ads only to earn rewards, because there are no rewards for ad clicks.

Log Data

Whenever you use the app, our service providers may collect data called Log Data. This may include your Internet Protocol (IP) address, device name, operating system version, the time and date of your use and other statistics. We use it to keep the app working and secure.

Cookies and Local Storage

The app stores small pieces of data in your browser, such as whether you have seen the tutorial, your referral code and which offers you have started. We do not use advertising cookies. Third-party services we use may set their own cookies, and you can refuse them in your browser settings.

Service Providers

We may employ third-party companies and individuals to:

  • Facilitate our service
  • Provide the service on our behalf
  • Perform service-related tasks
  • Help us analyse how the service is used

These third parties have access to your information only to perform these tasks and are obliged not to disclose or use it for any other purpose.

Data Retention and Deletion

We keep your information while your account is active. You can ask us to delete your account and data at any time by emailing sunshinerewards076@gmail.com from your registered email. We delete your data within 30 days. Any unpaid balance is forfeited when the account is deleted. We may keep limited records where the law requires or to prevent fraud.

Security

We value your trust and use commercially reasonable means to protect your information, including Google and Firebase security. No method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. We never ask for your password or OTP. Do not share them with anyone.

Links to Other Sites

The app contains links to partner websites and apps and to Telegram and WhatsApp. If you click a third-party link you will be taken to that site. We do not operate these sites, so please review their privacy policies.

Children's Privacy

Sunshine Rewards is meant for users aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If we find that a child has given us personal information, we delete it from our servers immediately.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Please review this page regularly. We will post any changes here and update the effective date above.

Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at sunshinerewards076@gmail.com.
